Our Services

More than just a meal

Of course when you think of Dumi's Kitchen you probably think of our legendary kotas and sizzling shisanyama. But did you know we offer so much more?

Take a look at what we offer below. Each service is delivered with the same love, quality and authenticity that has made us a Tzaneen institution. If you don't see what you are looking for, please do not hesitate to contact us - we are always happy to create a custom solution for you!

What we offer

In-Store Dining and Takeaway

The heart of our business - our daily service. Visit us at Stand no. 15 Rwanda Village, N'wamitwa. We offer both sit down didning at our outdoor benches and a fast convenient takeaway service for those on the go.

Catering for events

Planning a special ocassion? Let Dumi's Kitchen take care of the food so you can focus on celebrating. We offer full-service catering for events of all sizes, from intimate family gatherings to large corporate functions. Our team will work with you to craete a menu that reflects your taste and budget, using our authentic South African recipes that always impress.

Custom Menu Creation

Have a special request or dietary requirement? We can create a custom menu just for you. Our chefs are skilled in creating dishes that cater to specific dietary needs, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and more. We will work with you to create a menu that meets your needs without compromising on flavor or authenticity.

Cooking workshops and Demonstrations

Have you ever wanted to learn how to make a perfect kota? Or master the art of shisanyama? Our Cooking workshops are a fun, hands-on experience that bring people together to learn the secrets of authentic South African cuisine.
