Our Story

Who exactly are we? Founded in 2021 by Ms Dumisani Shipalana is Dumi's Kitchen, a well beloved local fast food establishment located in the deep rural areas of N'wamitwa in Tzaneen, South Africa. It is well known for serving authentic, home-style South African cuisine. The business started as a small garage stall selling food to locals and workers in the area and has now grown into a popular takeaway spot known famousely for its juicy kotas and its mouth-watery shisanyama. It now stands as people's most favoured shisanyama spot.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring home to every customer, with food that is hearty, affotdable and made with love. We are committed to preserving the rich culinary heritage of South Africa by using the traditional recipes that have been passed down through generations. Every ingridient we use is sourced fresh from local markets and farmers, ensuring that our meals are not only delicious but also support the local economy. We cook with the same care and passion that Ms Dumi has poured into every meal since she started her small garage stall in 2021 - no shortcuts, no compromises, only quality food and love!
