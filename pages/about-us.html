Our Story

Who exactly are we? Founded in 2021 by Ms Dumisani Shipalana is Dumi's Kitchen, a well beloved local fast food establishment located in the deep rural areas of N'wamitwa in Tzaneen, South Africa. It is well known for serving authentic, home-style South African cuisine. The business started as a small garage stall selling food to locals and workers in the area and has now grown into a popular takeaway spot known famousely for its juicy kotas and its mouth-watery shisanyama. It now stands as people's most favoured shisanyama spot.
