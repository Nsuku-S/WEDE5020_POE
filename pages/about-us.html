Our Story

Who exactly are we? Founded in 2021 by Ms Dumisani Shipalana is Dumi's Kitchen, a well beloved local fast food establishment located in the deep rural areas of N'wamitwa in Tzaneen, South Africa. It is well known for serving authentic, home-style South African cuisine. The business started as a small garage stall selling food to locals and workers in the area and has now grown into a popular takeaway spot known famousely for its juicy kotas and its mouth-watery shisanyama. It now stands as people's most favoured shisanyama spot.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring home to every customer, with food that is hearty, affotdable and made with love. We are committed to preserving the rich culinary heritage of South Africa by using the traditional recipes that have been passed down through generations. Every ingridient we use is sourced fresh from local markets and farmers, ensuring that our meals are not only delicious but also support the local economy. We cook with the same care and passion that Ms Dumi has poured into every meal since she started her small garage stall in 2021 - no shortcuts, no compromises, only quality food and love! Our mission is not all about profit - it is about purpose. It is abour honouring our past, feeding our present, and nourishing our future.

Every plate we serve is a promise - a promise of quality, heritage, and of love!

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the most recognised and trusted food brand in South Africa, celebrating our heritage through every meal we serve. We envision a future where Ma Dumi's Kitchen is a household name across the country - a symbol of authenticity, quality, and community that resonates with people from all walks of life. We dream of a South Africa where our children are proud of their heritage, and where the taste of a kota is as iconic as the sound of a vuvuzela.

We see a future where Dumi's Kitchen is not just a shisanyama but a cultural landmark - a place where people come to learn about South African cuisine, to celebrate their heritage, and to share meals with family and friends.
